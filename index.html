A local builder you can actually get hold of

Weston Built is run by Scott Weston out of Feilding. You deal with Scott from the first walk-through to the last coat of paint, so nothing gets lost between an office and the site.

Every job starts with a straight conversation about what you want, what it will cost and how long it will take. Then the work gets done the way it was quoted.

Exhibition stands and display builds

Weston Built contracts to businesses right across New Zealand, including Canterbury-based Prestige Design NZ, building the stands you walk into at the big field days.

Prestige Design service events from Gore to Auckland. Every booth is custom designed around the business it belongs to, then wrapped in high quality printed graphics.

Furniture and custom wool-press benches are built in the workshop and trucked to the event. Everything from the flooring to the lighting is installed on site by Prestige.

  • Custom booths designed per business
  • Benches, counters and bar leaners built to order
  • Flooring, walls and lighting installed on site
  • Packed down and away when the event finishes
